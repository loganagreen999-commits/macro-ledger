IF YOU WANT TO CHANGE SOMETHING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Drop a replacement into Desktop/ShamuStopper with the same filename and
say so; it will be processed and wired the same way. Sprites want a
transparent background and no baked-in shadow.

One thing that is still an approximation: the typeface. Currently
Pixelify Sans (Google Fonts, OFL) self-hosted at pixel/pixelify-400.woff2
and -700.woff2. It is close to the sheet but not the same face. If you
have the original, drop in a woff2 and the @font-face at the top of
pixel/pixel.css will pick it up.
