THE PROMPT
----------------------------------------------------------------
Create a single PNG asset sheet of 16 individual pixel-art icons for
a mobile nutrition app with a retro handheld-RPG look. Original art
only — do not copy or trace any existing game's artwork.

BACKGROUND: fully transparent. No white, no checkerboard, no colour
behind anything. This is the single most important requirement.

LAYOUT: a 4 x 4 grid. Each icon centred in its own cell with at least
40px of empty space around it. Print its filename in small plain white
text directly under each icon. Do not draw boxes, frames or borders
around the cells.

STYLE: crisp pixel art, roughly 2px black outline (#0A1011) around every
shape, flat fills with one lighter tint for a highlight and one darker
tint for shade. No gradients, no soft shadows, no anti-aliased edges,
no glow. Read clearly at 24 pixels tall.

PALETTE — use only these:
  outline    #0A1011      cream      #F6F2E5
  deep teal  #0B3940      mid teal   #0C6760
  bright grn #4AC089      light grn  #9EDAA0
  mint       #EDF3EF      slate      #3B4950
  red        #E8524A      yellow     #E8C33C
  blue       #4AA3E8      orange     #F2B84B
  pink       #D46FA8      brown      #8A5A3C
